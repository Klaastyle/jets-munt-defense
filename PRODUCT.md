# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Defense, aerospace and UAV program teams evaluating compact turbojet propulsion, electronics and integration support. They arrive with technical constraints, procurement risk and a need to trust the manufacturer before starting a conversation.

## Product Purpose
Position JetsMunt as a European specialist in compact UAV turbojet propulsion: engines, ECU/telemetry, integration and custom development. Success means the site feels like a serious engineering manufacturer, not a hobby turbine shop.

## Brand Personality
Precise, industrial, European. The interface should feel engineered, direct and tactile, with enough visual depth to make the technology tangible.

## Anti-references
Avoid hobby ecommerce, generic SaaS dark mode, startup gradients, decorative card grids, and anything that makes JetsMunt feel like a reseller instead of a manufacturer.

## Design Principles
Show the machinery first.
Lead with technical confidence, not marketing fluff.
Make specifications scannable and useful.
Use imagery and motion to explain engineering depth.
Keep the tone premium, restrained and operational.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Aim for WCAG AA contrast, legible technical copy, clear focus states and reduced-motion fallbacks for scroll and hover animation.
